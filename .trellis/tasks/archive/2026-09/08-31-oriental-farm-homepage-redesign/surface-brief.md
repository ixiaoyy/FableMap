# Homepage Surface Brief

## Scope and Mode

- Target: public `/` startup surface in `apps/mirror-island/client/src/App.vue`.
- Mode: Experience with an Operate-critical action layer.

## Audience, Job, and Action

- Audience: a visitor arriving to start or resume a private local farm without an account.
- Job: understand the world, confirm local-save status, then choose a new journey or continue safely.
- Primary action: start a new local world.
- Secondary action: continue the existing browser save when available.

## Content and Constraints

- Real content only: Mirror Island identity, local-world positioning, IndexedDB persistence status, clear-site-data risk, and credits link.
- Preserve initializing, menu, character creation, loading, playing, and error states.
- No login, cloud-save, multiplayer, server dependency, or baked-in image text.
- Media must use the reviewed immutable CDN/manifest path and keep image binaries out of Git.

## Chosen Direction

- An entering-the-scroll ritual: a luminous Oriental farm landscape surrounds a vertical paper-and-timber launch panel.
- Memorable moment: the page settles like an opened hanging scroll, while the path through the farm visually leads from the controls into the world.

## Unresolved Decisions

- None blocking. The supplied reference locks the direction; generated art remains original and text-free.
